## ⚡ PowerScope 1.1.0

### 🧭 A reorganized Charging page
- ⬆️ **What you check most comes first**: a new **Now** card (charge state, battery power, negotiated contract, input power, system load, time to full, PD link status), then **Live Voltages**, then **PD Link**
- 📁 **Details are collapsible**: Charger (with its PDOs), Contract Cross-check, Consistency Checks, Ports, Voltage Sources, and Charge Control and Raw Codes
- 🏷️ **Collapsed cards still tell you their state** in one line, such as "5 of 5 agree" or "6 passed · 1 skipped", and turn amber when something is off
- 💾 **Open or closed is remembered** per card across launches; collapsed cards are not built at all
- ✅ **Contract Cross-check** is now its own card and collapsed by default

### 🧹 Clear PD issues
- 🗑️ **Clear…** in the PD Link card deletes the timeline and restarts the issue counts, after a confirmation
- 📏 The chip's counters cannot be reset, so their values at the clear become a baseline; Controller counters show counts since the clear with the chip totals in brackets
- 📈 History charts and CSV export of history are unaffected

### ✨ Smaller changes
- 🔇 The short event list skips "Monitoring started" markers; Show all still includes them
- 🔤 Raw contract codes (RDO, PDO, basis) moved into Charge Control and Raw Codes

### 🧪 Tests
- 58 tests, including counter baselines across a clear and a controller reset

### 📦 Install
1. Download `PowerScope-v1.1.0.zip` and unzip it
2. Move `PowerScope.app` to `/Applications`
3. Clear the download quarantine once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PowerScope.app`
