## ⚡ PowerScope 1.2.0

### ⚡ A clearer Now card
- 🔢 **Three large readings**: what the charger delivers (blue), the negotiated contract, and battery power (green while charging, by speed)
- 📊 **Power budget bar**: the charger's contract as one bar, split into system load and battery charging, with the headroom left; amber with "Battery adds N W" when the charger is not enough
- 🧹 **Less noise**: no repeated status text, no "—" rows, no separate fast-charging row; secondary values are one quiet line
- 🔋 **On battery**, the card shows time remaining, system load and battery power instead

### 🧭 A clearer Overview
- 🔋 **At a glance**: a larger charge ring, the state, time to full or remaining as the headline, and a compact power budget bar
- 🗂️ **Three grouped cards** replace eight equal tiles: charge (mAh of full), health (with cycles and status) and temperature (with the lifetime range), each with a bar; voltages and currents now live only on the Charging page
- 🧹 The fast-charging rule caption and the long charger line are gone

### 🔗 Fairer PD statistics
- 🔌 **Plugging in is not an issue**: an isolated re-attach and its handshake (renegotiation, a rejected first request, a brief contract loss) fold into one "Charger attached" event that is not counted
- ⚠️ **A flaky link still counts**: a second re-attach within 2 minutes, and everything after it, are issues
- 🔕 **Unplugging is not an issue**: losing the contract because external power went away is "Charger removed"

### 🎯 Accuracy
- 🔋 **Live battery current and power**: they now come from the SMC (`B0AC`, every second) instead of the gauge, which refreshes only every few to tens of seconds. Right after plugging back in, the gauge still reported −1.47 A for over 30 s while the battery was idle; that no longer shows as "battery assisting". An energy-balance check falls back to the gauge if the SMC's direction ever disagrees.

### 🐛 Fixes
- 📏 The History page's time-range picker no longer wraps its label letter by letter in a narrow window; it becomes a menu instead
- 🔁 The Overview headline no longer repeats the status badge

### 🧪 Tests
- 68 tests, including the real 100 W plug-in, the failing cable's repeated re-attaches, and the stale gauge current

### 📦 Install
1. Download `PowerScope-v1.2.0.zip` and unzip it
2. Move `PowerScope.app` to `/Applications`
3. Clear the download quarantine once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PowerScope.app`
