## ⚡ PowerScope 1.0.0

The first release: a native macOS battery and USB-C Power Delivery monitor.

### 🔌 Charging and USB-C PD
- 🤝 Negotiated contract resolved from the power manager, the PMU setpoint and the port controller RDO, including EPR 28 V
- 🧮 Decoded source capabilities (Fixed, PPS, AVS) per USB PD 3.1
- ✅ Cross-checks against every place macOS reports the contract
- 📡 Voltage sources labeled as measured, setpoint or derived, plus consistency checks
- 🔗 PD link health and timeline: reconnects, hard resets, renegotiations and rejects
- 🔌 Ports view: physical USB-C and MagSafe inputs and every port controller

### 🔋 Battery
- 💚 Health, cycles, temperature, cell groups and identity

### 📈 History and export
- ⏱️ 1 s readings stored per 5 s window (average, min, max) for 7 days
- 📤 CSV export of history and PD events (File menu or the History page)

### 🧭 App
- 🪟 Main window and menu bar panel; keeps sampling in the background at about 1.8 % of one core
- 🎨 One color rule: green for charging, neutral for normal, amber for attention, red for action

### 📦 Install
1. Download `PowerScope-v1.0.0.zip` and unzip it
2. Move `PowerScope.app` to `/Applications`
3. The app is signed ad hoc, so clear the download quarantine once: `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/PowerScope.app`

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple Silicon.
