## 🎨 PowerScope 1.3.0

### ⭕ A two-ring gauge
- 🔋 **Outer ring = charge level**, colored by level on a continuous scale: red at 0–10 %, orange at 20 %, yellow at 40 %, yellow-green at 60 %, green when full. A full battery is now a complete green ring, not a gray one
- ⚡ **Inner ring = charging speed**, the power going into the battery on a fixed 0–100 W scale, so different chargers compare directly. Its color sweeps from pale chartreuse (trickle) through green, mint, teal and cyan to a bright sky blue at 100 W, more vivid the faster it charges
- ⚠️ When the battery helps a charger that is not enough, the inner ring turns amber and runs backwards
- 🏷️ The center shows the power and tier under the percentage, for example "⚡ 45 W · Fast"; the menu bar panel uses the same two rings

### 🚀 Speed tiers
- Trickle < 10 W, charging 10–30 W, fast 30–60 W, and a new **very fast ≥ 60 W**
- The status badge and the Now card's battery figure use the speed color

### 📊 A readable power budget bar
- 🏷️ A legend under the bar everywhere: ● Mac ● Battery ○ Spare
- 🧹 The repeated "Charger · Battery" line on the Overview is gone; it now shows the PD contract voltage instead

- 81 tests, adding the level and speed scales, speed tiers, the inner ring and the budget legend
